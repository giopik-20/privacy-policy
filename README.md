## Privacy and Data Protection

Cake Calculator does not use analytics, advertising, third-party tracking tools, or external servers.

All information entered into the app is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted elsewhere.

## Sharing Recipes

Cake Calculator allows users to share information with other users and to receive recipes, currently through QR codes.

Cake Calculator does not verify the origin, accuracy, or safety of shared information and accepts no responsibility for information exchanged between users. Always make sure that recipes you receive come from a trustworthy source. You are responsible for the information you choose to share.

Recipes stored in QR codes are not cryptographically protected and may be read, copied, or modified by others. Do not treat QR codes as a secure way to protect confidential information.

## Recipe Database and Data Changes

The recipe database included in Cake Calculator may be modified, updated, reorganized, or replaced over time. Recipes may be added, changed, or removed without notice.

Cake Calculator does not guarantee the permanent availability, integrity, or preservation of any particular recipe or database content.
